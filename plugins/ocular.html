OCULAR is an innovative SAP Business One plugin developed by Vestrics Solutions to address the unique needs of optical retail and wholesale businesses.
From managing optometric prescriptions to point-of-sale (POS) operations, OCULAR seamlessly integrates with SAP Business One to provide end-to-end support for your optical operations.
