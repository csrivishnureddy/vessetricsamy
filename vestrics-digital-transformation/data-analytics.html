Organizations that adopt data-driven decision-making are 23 times more likely to acquire customers, six times more likely to retain them, and 19 times more likely to achieve profitability.

The global big data analytics market is projected to grow to $349.56 billion by 2024, driven by advancements in predictive and prescriptive analytics.
