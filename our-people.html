It’s About Growing Together

At Vestrics Solutions, our people are the foundation of everything we do. From visionary leaders to skilled professionals, our people bring expertise, creativity, and a customer-first approach to every challenge.
We are proud of the strong culture and values that guide us - where collaboration, integrity, and innovation come together to deliver impactful solutions.

Stories From the People Who
Make Vestrics

Behind every solution we deliver is the dedication of our incredible team. Here’s what some of
them have to say about their journey at Vestrics:

Your first job is always precious and respectful.

The past six years at Vestrics have been a journey of growth and learning. As an SAP partner company, Vestrics offers a dynamic environment where innovation thrives and ideas are valued. I’ve gained valuable experience working on impactful SAP projects while enjoying a supportive team culture.

With opportunities for continuous learning and vibrant celebrations, Vestrics is more than just a workplace—it’s a place to grow, collaborate, and succeed.

~ Prudhvi Prasanna Ram Ravipati

I’ve been a Project Manager at Vestrics Solutions Pvt. Ltd. for four years.

It’s been an incredible journey of growth and learning. When I joined, I started managing smaller projects but quickly transitioned to leading complex initiatives thanks to my collaboration and problem-solving skills.

One of my proudest achievements was leading a team to deliver an SAP Rollout-based solution for a key client, improving their workflow efficiency by 30%.

The innovative and supportive environment at Vestrics has helped me grow professionally and personally. I’m excited to take on new challenges and contribute to the company’s success.

~ Vivekananda Reddy Ankilla

Hi, I’m Rani, a Senior Software Engineer at Vestrics Solutions.

Over the past four years, I’ve grown tremendously. Today, I lead a team of four, conduct training sessions, manage projects, and ensure smooth delivery by completing projects on time. At Vestrics, I’ve learned the art of multitasking, maintaining work-life balance, and meeting deadlines. Vestrics isn’t just a workplace; it’s a place of growth, recognition, and a positive work culture. I’ve received awards like Best Performance (2024) and accolades in competitions.

From being a nervous fresher to a confident Senior Software Engineer, my journey at Vestrics has been truly rewarding!

~ Rani NVSN

Vestrics is a place of growth, learning, and camaraderie.

Over the past five years, I’ve had the privilege of driving business growth and optimization for our clients. I’m grateful to work alongside an incredible team of dedicated professionals who inspire me every day.

My amazing teammates have been instrumental in our shared successes, bringing their unique perspectives, expertise, and enthusiasm to every project. Their support has made even the most challenging projects enjoyable and rewarding.Here’s to many more years of driving business transformation and excellence!

~ Amar Athmuri
